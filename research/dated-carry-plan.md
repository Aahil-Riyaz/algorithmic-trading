# Quarterly cash-and-carry experiment

Request each March, June, September and December BTC and ETH linear USDT contract with expiry from March 2024 to September 2026 inclusive. Use USD-M perpetuals only in the separate funding study. For dated contracts, at 08:00 UTC seven calendar days before the scheduled last-Friday 08:00 expiry, compare the last completed five-minute futures and spot closes. Each asset has a fixed 5,000 USDT sleeve inside a 10,000 USDT account. Size each hedge to at most 40% of sleeve equity per leg, using equal units. Enter if the observed futures premium covers estimated entry, delivery and spot-sale costs, a 4% simple annual hurdle on the full sleeve for seven days, and an additional 5 bp of spot notional. Compare with entering all available contracts on the same dates and with cash. The archive is a trade-price proxy; require traded bars. Use the next five-minute close as entry. Hold to actual published delivery price. Sell spot at its last five-minute close at expiry. The future settles against an index average, which may differ from the spot sale. Charge 10 bp spot per transaction, 5 bp futures entry and delivery fee, plus 2 bp execution slippage on each of the three market trades. These are research costs, not an account fee quote. No funding on dated futures. Mark an expiry missing if the delivery record cannot be corroborated; do not manufacture settlement as the spot price.

Keep 2024–25 development results separate from the later 2026 deliveries. Show all qualifying, skipped and unavailable pairs, net dollars, portfolio return, idle cash, excess over the hypothetical cash yield, sensitivity to extra execution cost and dependence on the most profitable expiry. The live quote check uses actual spot asks and futures bids at size; its maturity horizon can differ from this seven-day experiment. No private endpoints or orders.

## Follow-up fixed after the seven-day comparison

The seven-day premium never cleared the entry hurdle. A structural alternative is to pay the round-trip fees once over most of a quarter. Add exactly one longer horizon, 84 calendar days before expiry. Use the same clock, two-coin mandate, signal, cost assumptions, capital, controls and later period. Carry the full 84-day capital charge into the entry rule. Use spot from the correct month on each leg. Date the first portfolio observation from January 1 in each period and exclude any contract whose entry precedes it. This is a follow-up on historical markets already examined. Freeze it before computing 84-day results; keep the seven-day result visible.
