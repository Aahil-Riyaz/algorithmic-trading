# Aahil Wave decision record

**Verdict: unknown durable edge.** The friend's pasted outcomes fail the fee-adjusted exchange replay. Our independently trained model lost money in 2025 validation. The evidence supports paper observation only.

## Findings in order of severity

1. **A posted win conflicts with the exchange path.** On November 5 the 23:01 UTC AVAXUSDT candle crossed the buy limit and protective stop without touching target. The target came in the following minute. Under immediate contract-price protection, changing that one reported TP to a stop erases about 2.5R before fees. Unknown venue, trigger source, queue and actual fills limit attribution. [Reconstruction, dates, candles and mismatch rates](discord-exchange-audit.md)
2. **The missing records change the population.** The actual attachment contains 54 alerts starting October 29. It has one cumulative observation, 136 closed, and two unresolved visible setups. The cumulative population predates the paste. There is no defensible reconstruction of an account curve or an earlier 124-trade snapshot from these records. The reported-close method gives +1.359R after mixed costs over 14 fills. The closest retrospective exchange reconstruction gives −1.61R on those same 14. A switch from +5R reported gross to a modest after-cost loss needs no exotic trading hypothesis. [Reported outcomes, group statistics and fees](discord-reported.md)
3. **Cancellation and break-even information arrives after the alert.** The later invalidation message supplies the Wave 1 level for 36 setups. Assuming the supplied level retrospectively and selecting a 0.5R break-even trigger matches 51/52 resolved updates. A trade-through replay using only submitted prices and fixed stops mismatches 39/50 comparable outcomes. The former is an explanatory fit. Neither reconstruction demonstrates prospective private decisions. All 54 posted prices were already beyond target in the signal direction before the required pullback. That measures entry distance and does not establish an earlier target fill.
4. **The pivot provenance cannot be recovered.** A specific falsification probe checks the last 48 hours of five-minute highs (long) or lows (short), matching the disclosed Wave 1 level within half its last printed unit. It requires a strict extremum versus two completed bars on each side. Five of 36 had a matching, already-confirmed pivot. For 31 there was no match. Zero had only a match whose confirmation fell after publication. The friend's timeframe, pivot timestamps and algorithms are absent. Multiple possible levels make even a match inconclusive. In our older `waves.py`, a centered pivot requires two later closes; a decision at confirmation is permissible but dating the decision to the extremum would leak. The new zigzag records separate extremum and confirmation indices, and has a prefix-invariance test. [Pivot probe](../audit_wave_pivots.py)
5. **Small wins and incompatible fee claims.** Reconstructed Aug/Sep results are +0.207R/fill with an i.i.d. bootstrap interval of −0.177 to +0.593R. Eight of fourteen winners were timeouts and six were target hits. Three fills made 225.48 of 247.77 USDT. Control lost 656.11 across 284 fills. The 1,195.11 USDT fee bill against 133.08 gross applies to a different March–April run, which also had 284 fills. The commit trail supports exposure to repeated historical periods, but does not prove the author's private order of gate selection. October's earlier classifier earned +82.63 USDT on nine fills, so “every gated variant lost” needs the variant identified. The expiring-quote variants did lose. [Ledger audit script](../audit_wave_claims.py) · [Earlier study audit](regime-audit.md)
6. **A more complex model still lost money.** Four quarterly 2025 forward folds produced 65 qualifying signals, six opportunities blocked by an existing position, 59 submissions, 34 fills and 12 wins. The combined model lost 7.61R, or 382.16 USDT across four separately funded accounts. Mean −0.224R/fill, seven-day block bootstrap 95% interval −0.666 to +0.167. The seeded timing control lost 15.08R. Zero-return cash beat both. BTC, ETH and SOL qualified each month; AVAX never reached the top three in this four-contract mandate. The conditional forecasts refer to standalone proposals; order replacement, inventory, and paper overlays change realized probabilities. [Policy ledger](aahil-wave/wave-orders.csv) · [Calibration and uncertainty](aahil-wave/uncertainty.json)
7. **Selection and execution remain major unknowns.** Thirty-four fills cannot justify choosing a confidence bucket, weekday, feature family or an alternative by its realized rank. Using six aligned strategies and eight calendar blocks, the best daily Sharpe was negative. Its illustrative deflated-Sharpe probability was 0.091. No Sharpe-based CSCV partition had positive return variance for every variant in both halves. Using mean daily R as a separate selection criterion gave PBO 0.343 on 70 partitions. These figures omit informal trials and treat fixed-risk exit-day R as returns. CPCV is a diagnostic which can train on subsequent dates, and is reported separately from forward-only validation. OHLC penetration cannot recover queue priority, partial executions, client latency, cancellation races or market impact. [Selection code](../wave_selection.py) · [Purged validation](aahil-wave/cpcv.csv) · [DSR](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/deflated-sharpe.pdf) · [PBO](https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf)

## Freeze and criteria for moving past paper

These are proposed decision criteria, not demonstrated live performance. The main paper policy adds invalidation, one-minute monitoring, a break-even move after a favorable 1R close, correlated caps and separate maker/taker cost assumptions. The historical training policy has its own exits and fee schedule. Calibrate against the exact paper policy and venue fee tier before using the public probability estimates for sizing. No trade is a valid outcome.

1. Freeze model, thresholds, costs, data receipt policy and output hashes before a new observation window. Reserve December 1–31, 2026 UTC for one sealed prospective check. Track outages and every qualifying, rejected, replaced, unfilled and open order. If the producer is offline or the rules change, label coverage and start a new freeze. Never backfill a prospective decision after its activation.
2. Collect at least 4–8 weeks of prospective paper observation and extend until sample criteria are met. Require positive lower 95% weekly-block bounds on both net R/submission and net R/fill after funding and stressed execution costs. Require maker queue/partial-fill bounds, reliable fill timestamps, matched replay/paper account and no unreconciled gaps. Predefine maximum differences of 0.10R/fill and 5 percentage points of fill rate on matched opportunities; these are proposed tolerances. A positive weekly mean alone is insufficient.
3. For planning, about 221 independent fills provide 80% power at one-sided 5% to distinguish 58.3% from 50%. Around 600 independent fills detect a fixed 5 USDT/fill at the old 49.23 USDT standard deviation under the same assumptions. Shared market shocks and overlapping labels reduce effective sample size. Different sizing changes the USDT calculation. The current tests cannot meet those criteria.
4. After passing, start a separately authorized minimum-size experiment with capped risk and reconciled exchange acknowledgements, fills, fee tiers, funding and markouts. Compare paper and actual execution on paired opportunities. Consider scaling only when net live expectancy clears zero, its confidence interval for the difference lies within the predeclared tolerance, and realized drawdown stays inside the frozen budget. Current decision: paper only.
