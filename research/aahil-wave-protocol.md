# Aahil Wave pooled research specification

Development dates are January 2024 through December 2025. December 2023 supplies the initial liquidity snapshot and feature history. Four expanding quarterly validation folds cover 2025. No configuration is selected on the walk-forward results. Reserve December 1–31, 2026 UTC for one prospective evaluation after the implementation, feature list, thresholds and cost assumptions have been frozen. Do not read, tune on, or claim results from that future period. A change to the rules during the period starts a new evaluation.

Restrict the mandate to BTC, ETH, SOL and AVAX linear USDT perpetuals. For each month rank by previous month's quote turnover after requiring a spread of at most 5 bp and at least 100,000 USDT on each side of the 1% band, then retain up to three. Spread uses the median of the first 512 KiB of compressed L1 quotes from the previous month's first day. Depth uses the median bilateral minimum across available hourly snapshots on that day. This small, fixed mandate and sparse monthly liquidity sampling limit generalization to other assets and exchanges.

At the hourly boundary use only completed five-minute candles. Track close-based zigzag extrema over the preceding 72 hours and confirm reversals only after a move of 2 rolling 14-bar ATR. The last five confirmed extrema supply a mechanical candidate side for the projected fifth leg. Wave-2 retracement of 38.2–78.6%, wave-4 retracement of 23.6–50%, extension, overlap and bars since confirmation are predictors. A confirmation at a later time cannot change an earlier emitted feature record.

Quote half an ATR away after one five-minute activation delay. Require two ATR or four modeled round-trip costs of stop distance, whichever is larger. Gross target is 1.5 stop distances. Simulate a post-only reject at a marketable activation open, a strict price-through fill, six-hour entry expiry, stop-first ambiguity, and 24-hour vertical exit after entry. Charge 10 bp each leg, 2 bp slippage on stops/time exits, plus signed Binance funding. The maker-only test uses an OHLC proxy. Real queue size, partial fills, tick/lot rounding and cancel races require exchange events. Store an unfilled proposal with zero R. R divides net per-unit return by modeled stop loss including both fees and stop slippage.

Fit pooled shallow XGBoost classifiers for fill across all candidates and TP-before-stop conditional on a fill. Each model gets a separate temporal logistic calibration on the latest 20% of development candidates, purging the preceding training labels plus a 24-hour embargo. The calibrated TP probability mixes empirically observed TP and non-TP average net payouts from the calibration period, including timeouts. Multiply this conditional payout by fill probability. Incremental submission cost in simulation is zero. Require expected net R above +0.05 per signal. Emit no signal below threshold.

Compare a fixed cumulative feature ladder to a random-time control. The production research candidate uses the price, wave, signed executed volume, same-venue spot basis/funding and lagged BTC return families. Sparse L1, OI and liquidation ablations use only common timestamps and report coverage. Metrics for sequential order policies use one position per coin and replace a pending entry on a new qualifying signal. Separate CPCV diagnostics assess hypothetical individual proposals. Use the same effective R, expiry, fill, stop, target and fee assumptions across controls.
