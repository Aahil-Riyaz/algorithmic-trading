# Expiring quote test

Freeze before evaluating October 2026. Fit six independent expected-net-R regressors on July–September, with a full day between the last resolved training outcome and October. Use the existing regime gates, fixed XGBoost depth 3, 100 trees, learning rate 0.04. Include expired proposals at zero payoff. Require predicted R above zero. Limit each entry to three five-minute bars from activation. A qualifying newer signal for the same coin cancels its remaining unfilled entry before the new quote activates. If already filled, keep the existing protective exits. Assume atomic cancellation at the five-minute boundary. Reject a new long whose limit is at or above the activation open as a proxy for post-only rejection. Keep the original stop, target, cost and funding assumptions. Use the same signals in keep-first and replace policies, repeat replacement with a two-basis-point depth-of-penetration requirement, and replay the earlier five-minute classifier as a benchmark. No October retuning.

## Research basis

- Avellaneda and Stoikov model how inventory and time affect a dealer's reservation price and quote widths. Their evidence is a model and simulation. That motivates a finite quote lifetime and an inventory constraint here. It does not establish a transferable crypto return. [Original paper](https://math.nyu.edu/inmemoriam/avellaneda/HighFrequencyTrading.pdf)
- Huang, Lehalle and Rosenbaum estimate how book state affects order arrivals, cancellations and executions. This motivates conditioning on visible liquidity and explicitly testing fill assumptions. Our five-minute archives cannot reconstruct a FIFO queue. [Queue-reactive model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1312.0563)
- Kolm, Turiel and Westray evaluated order-flow forecasts on 115 Nasdaq stocks. Their results favor stationary order-flow inputs and short forecasting horizons. Here depth and aggressive volume ratios are inputs to a net-payoff target, with unfilled proposals included. Transfer to crypto and profitability after costs require separate testing. [Published research](https://doi.org/10.1111/mafi.12413)
- Queue position has economic value. A refreshed price may improve relevance while sacrificing time priority. The keep-first comparison measures this policy change only within the bar simulator. [Moallemi and Yuan](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/pubfiles/25461/queue-value-2016.pdf)
