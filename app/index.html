Trend TP/SL Simulator

Long trades only while the 20/60/120-day trend signal is fully on. Half off at TP1, then a trailing stop. Daily Binance spot candles. Paper only: this page cannot place orders.
This is a historical simulation. Changing the settings and keeping whatever looks best on past data is curve-fitting. The defaults are the rules that were fixed and confirmed before scoring; see research/trend-tpsl-stops. A 20x setting is not offered because these stops are usually wider than a 20x liquidation distance.
